# Facts still to verify

Nothing here has been replayed against the game by the author. The content was drafted from the community pages linked in each chapter and group, and is guidance, not an audit.

## Counts

The completion targets (`targets` in `src/guide/config.ts`) are pinned by a test: 127 Echoes, 40 Pieces of Heart, 7 Heart Containers, 150 Might Crystals (by amount), 50 side quests, 25 stamp stands, 28 accessory versions, 8 outfits, 69 recipes, 6 automatons and 4 Fairy Bottles, which are game totals with the hosts below, and the guide's own lists, whose goals are their rows: 57 dungeon chests, 12 mini-dungeon chests, 38 overworld chests, 4 equipment, 2 abilities, 11 upgrades, 2 milestones, 22 minigame entries and 43 Waypoints. The test checks the data against those numbers, not the numbers against the game.

- **Accessories:** 28 versions are listed, but two earlier accessories are replaced by upgraded forms, so a finished save holds 26 distinct ones. The 28 comes from the source list and is not checked in-game.
- **Overworld chests** (`overworld-chest`, a second tag on the `chest` rows that are not dungeon or mini-dungeon ones and award nothing the board counts elsewhere, so a dungeon chest is never also an overworld one) have no official total: Console Pulse says the game has no counter and Gamer Guides says only "several". Only the chests the routes pass are listed: 46 carry `chest` outside the dungeons, and the 8 that award a Fairy Bottle, Might Crystals or an accessory (a bottle, a crystal chest and six accessory chests) count once, as that item, and no longer carry `overworld-chest`, so the target's goal is 38. Two dungeon chests (`chest-suthorn-ruins-heart-pin`, `chest-castle-frog-ring`) award an accessory and count once, as that accessory (the 28 is a game total that needs every row), so they carry neither `dungeon-chest` nor `overworld-chest`: a guide's own list never recounts a row another target counts. The dungeon chests' goal, the guide's own sum, was 59 until 19 Nov 2026 and is 57.
- **Waypoints** (`waypoint`, the guide's own list, goal 43: 17 overworld rows tagged `waypoint` and 26 inside dungeons tagged `dungeon-waypoint` and then `waypoint`) count since 19 Nov 2026, the goal being the rows. There is no game total and the game shows no progress for them: [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Waypoint) says they unlock warping from the map, [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/is-there-a-legend-of-zelda-echoes-of-wisdom-post-game/) says the map's progress shows only Stamps, Pieces of Heart and Might Crystals, and Console Pulse says there is no counter, percentage or reward. Nintendo Everything lists "All Waypoints" in its 100% checklist but says nothing in the game shows which are missing. Third-party totals disagree (Game Rant 41 overworld, Gamer Guides 42 overworld plus 7 Still World), none counts dungeon ones, and the guide's 17 overworld rows cover under half of them, so the 43 are the Waypoints the routes pass, not every one in the game: a reader who finds another has no row to tick, and a bar at 100% does not mean every Waypoint is active. `waypoint-stilled-castle-town` and `waypoint-stilled-faron` have a `waypoint-` id but carry the `dungeon-waypoint` tag.
- **Echoes learned in Slumber Dojo dreams** are temporary, so the Echo entries expect you to learn those monsters again outside.

## What the bar counts beyond the game's own lists

The first eleven targets come with game totals (above), and the dungeon chests and mini-dungeon chests are sums of the guide's rows (above). The rest are the guide's own lists, whose goals are their rows (core's `data.test.ts` holds each to its tag's rows), so no total is claimed for them; what the sources do say:

- **Equipment (4):** the Tri Rod and the Sword, Bow and Bombs of Might. [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Swordfighter_Form) and [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/477095) give the three weapons, and [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Tri_Rod) the Tri Rod; no source states a total.
- **Abilities (2):** Bind and Reverse Bond, the two Tri teaches in Suthorn Ruins ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Echoes_of_wisdom), [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/471933)). Zelda Wiki's category of abilities has 21 pages (Jumping, Swimming and Swordfighter Form among them), so there is no wider game total.
- **Upgrades (11):** the nine Lueburry upgrades match two hosts ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Lueburry), Game8 477095): energy to level 4 for 5, 10 and 20 Might Crystals, and the Sword, Bow and Bombs to level 3 for 10 and 20, with all upgrades costing 125 crystals and the last 25 giving the free energy refill; levels 3 and 4 only unlock after "Still Missing", which the rows don't say. The other two are four accessory slots (100, 300, 500 and 1,000 rupees on [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Great_Fairies) and [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/476315); Nintendo Life says 200 for the third, an outlier) and `upgrade-energy-recharger`, the pot Lueburry makes from the last 25 crystals to refill Zelda's energy. No page gives it a name the row could use: Zelda Wiki calls it a Pot ("Might Crystal Pot" in a caption), and Game8 and other guides a Statue, so the row says pot and mentions statue. The group cited a Nintendo Life upgrade page with no prices and now cites Zelda Wiki and Game8. Game8's per-level cells did not read, only its totals (125 crystals for all upgrades, 35 for energy, 30 for each weapon, and 1,900 rupees for the slots), which fit the rows.
- **Milestones (2):** Tri level 11 is the maximum ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Tri%27s_Power), Nintendo Life, Nintendo Everything, which adds that the optional rifts are needed). The clear star is said by Console Pulse only ("adds a star"); Siliconera says only "a new notation".
- **Minigames (22):** a row count: 39 rows carry `minigame` (15 Slumber Dojo "fast" rows, 8 Dojo reward rows, 5 Acorn, 4 Flag Race, 4 Mango Rush, 3 race and glide quest rows), and the 17 that also award a Piece of Heart, Might Crystals, an accessory, an outfit or a side quest already count under those targets, so `minigame-extra`, a second tag on the other 22, is what counts. Sourced sub-totals: 15 Dojo trials with cumulative rewards at 2, 4, 6, 8, 11 and 14 ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Battle_Challenge), Thonky and Nintendo Life), 5 Acorn Gathering courses (Nintendo Life, Game8, Console Pulse), 3 Flag Race courses (Zelda Wiki, Console Pulse) and 3 Mango Rush seeds (Zelda Wiki, Console Pulse). No source gives one minigame total.

Found while counting:

- **Slumber Dojo fast-goal times** (read on 2026-11-19). The game's Dojo master gives each trial a goal time and a faster hidden one, which [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Battle_Challenge) (citing the game's own data, "BattleChallenge v1.0.1") calls the hidden goal time and [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-echoes-of-wisdom-slumber-dojo-all-challenges-and-rewards) the Fast Clear. The rows' times came from the "Fast Clear" column of a GameFAQs page, which looks like its author's own clear times ("<15" for Flame and Ice, "<10:25" for the last Titans) and not the game's thresholds, so the rows were changed on 19 Nov 2026. The two hosts agree on seven trials: Caromadillos 0:45 (Game8 too), Wind 0:50, Titans 5:00, Floating 1:30 and Flame and Ice 0:40 now read so, and Flow 1:10 and Final Blank 1:30 were right. On the other eight the hosts disagree (Zelda Wiki / Nintendo Life): Blank-Slate 0:40 / 0:45, Moblins 0:35 / 0:30, Shock 1:15 / 1:00, Ice 1:25 / 1:20, Skies 0:55 / 0:30, Wizzrobe 2:10 / 1:30 (Game8 says 1:30 too), Titans Again 8:45 / 9:00 and Final Titans 13:50 / 13:30. Each of those rows gives both times and says to beat the faster, which clears the trial either way. Not read: the Zelda Dungeon wiki and Destructoid (Cloudflare check); Game8's figures for Blank-Slate Final and Titans Again are search summaries only. Only a clear in the game, or its Dojo menu, settles the eight.
- **Acorn Gathering** (read on 2026-11-19): five courses, and the times and rewards in the rows match [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-echoes-of-wisdom-all-acorn-gathering-locations) and [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/Acorn-Gathering) for all five: Castle Town under 17 seconds for 3 Might Crystals, Kakariko under 25 for a Heart Piece, far west under 40 for the Steel Trap, Jabul under 25 for 120 rupees on the first clear, and the Northern Sanctuary under 25 for a Golden Egg. The acorn counts are split, so the rows dropped the three that one source backs: Kakariko (Nintendo Life 10, xboxplay.games 11), far west (Nintendo Life 11, Siliconera and xboxplay.games 16) and the Sanctuary (Nintendo Life 11, xboxplay.games perhaps 10). Castle Town's ten and Jabul's six are in Nintendo Life and in xboxplay.games, a weak aggregator. Zelda Wiki's Acorn Gathering page is a stub, and Destructoid and Zelda Dungeon were not read (403 and a bot check).
- **The Castle Town acorn crystal** is settled: the clear under 17 seconds awards 3 ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/Might-Crystal-Locations) "x3", Nintendo Life "3 Might Crystals", and Zelda Wiki's table, whose quantity comes before its own place, which the earlier note had paired with the row after), and the crystal on the castle roof is its own entry worth 1 (`crystal-castle-roof`), so the 150 stands.

## Missables

Game8 ([list of missables and one-time events](https://game8.co/games/Zelda-Echoes-of-Wisdom/archives/473539)) says the game has no missables, and Console Pulse ([100% completion hub](https://consolepulse.com/nintendo/zelda/guides/zelda-echoes-of-wisdom-complete-guide-100-percent-hub)) says nothing becomes permanently missable and that a cleared save reloads before the ending. Both pages were read directly on 2026-11-14. That counts as two sources, but neither is an official statement, and a single reader report mentions missing items on a first pass, which is consistent with coming back. Game8 also lists one-time events that are not missables: chests open once, quests can't be replayed, and some rifts close after the story but hold no collectibles.

`src/guide/data/missables.ts` is therefore empty, and each chapter shows a one-line note saying nothing is flagged. If a real point of no return turns up with two independent sources, add it there and the "Don't miss" list replaces the note.

## Content not carried over

- The original chapter checklists duplicated the guide steps, so they were not ported as separate lists; the same steps are the entries.
- Five minigame bullets in the completion list (Slumber Dojo, Mango Rush, Flag Race, Acorn Gathering, Goron Race and gliding) repeat entries that already exist as route or sweep steps, so they were not added again; those entries are the `minigame` rows; the ones that award nothing the board counts already (`minigame-extra`) count as a target since 19 Nov 2026.
- Completion-list items derivable from other entries (all Echoes, all hearts, waypoints, chests, Fairy Bottles, accessory slots, final 25 crystals) were not duplicated; two upgrade lines matched existing steps.

## Map links

Each stamp row links to its own "Stamp Stand #n" heading on Nintendo Life's stamp page, read from its HTML on 2026-11-18. The page describes each stand in a line and the rows were written from other pages, so six rows were matched to their heading by region and by elimination among that region's stands: Gerudo's northwest plateau (#8), the islet south of Hebra (#9), the southwest corner of Zora River (#13), the snowy ledge west of the Moblin camp (#20), the ledge above the paired torches (#24) and the far east Eldin glide (#4). Check them in the game if a reader reports one that lands on the wrong stand. The page's own numbers move if it is edited.
