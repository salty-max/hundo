## Counts

The game keeps no completion figure: none of RPG Site's features list, Realm of Darkness's FAQ or Gamer Guides' hubs names an in-game item count, bestiary or percentage. Its own records are its fixed lists. Realm of Darkness's FAQ (#9, #11, #14, #64) states the site's totals: 40 medals, 22 scrolls, 15 Secret Spots with 41 treasures, 48 sparkly spots with 134 treasures, and 116 monsters, without saying the game counts them. The monsters aren't counted here: no in-game record of them was found, and defeating them is grinding, not finding. Where the second host below is RPG Site, its list is counted (numbered to 40, 22 entries, 15 spots) rather than stated.

## Achievements: a source never shown

No page read names a list of achievements kept inside the game: RPG Site's features list, Realm of Darkness's FAQ and Gamer Guides' achievement hubs list 48 achievements for Steam, PlayStation and Xbox only, and Switch has no platform achievements. So the guide shows none ([chapter-data.md](../../../docs/chapter-data.md#achievements)). Steam's list served as a completeness check: the bosses, the five sigils, the recipes. What would change this: two hosts naming an in-game list.
