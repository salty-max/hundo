# Facts to verify

What this guide claims that nobody has confirmed in the game. Nothing here was replayed. The inventory of every secret was parsed raw on 24 November 2026 from the wiki's achievements table (bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg, its MediaWiki API) and Steam's achievement list, and checked against tboi.com (Platinum God), strategywiki and a Dead God route on Steam. The full inventory, with each secret's hosts and every disagreement, is `isaac-inventory.json` in the research notes the guide was planned from.

The fandom wiki is a fork of wiki.gg, so the two count as one source. isaacguru.com's data is written in the wiki's own template code, so it counts as a copy of the wiki, not a second host.

## Conditions on one host

143 of the 641 conditions rest on the wiki alone (most co-op babies and challenge unlocks, the tainted Mega Satan pickups, some Booster Pack items and donation tiers). Their rows are drafted from the wiki's condition. Phase 2 checks them chapter by chapter against a second host; any that finds none is listed here by number.
