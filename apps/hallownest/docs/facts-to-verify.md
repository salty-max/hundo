## The game's own achievements

Since 19 November 2026 a guide shows achievements only when the game keeps a list of its own, so every reader has one whatever they play on; a platform's list alone (Steam, Xbox, PlayStation) can check the data but is never shown (core's `data.test.ts`). Hollow Knight passes, so the Pure Completion row and its board line stay, called the game's achievements (the row says the game lists them itself on Switch; the label says only "Achievement", since nothing confirms an in-game list on PlayStation or Xbox), and `config.ts` carries the sources below in `achievements`.

- **On Switch, which has no achievements of its own, the game shows them itself.** [wiki Achievements](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Achievements_(Hollow_Knight)) (read in a browser; automated fetches get a 403): in-game "Feats", named "Quests" before version 1.5.12459. [Rogue Ranker](https://rogueranker.com/hollow-knight-achievements/): listed in-game as Feats. [fextralife trophy guide](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Trophy_and_Achievement_Guide): called Quests on Switch, the older name. A Nintendo Life forum thread (2019) and a ResetEra thread also name Hollow Knight among Switch games with in-game achievements.
- **On PC the game has an achievement browser of its own.** Two GOG forum threads ("no achievements", "help with ingame achievements") describe it showing an empty list until a Galaxy file conflict is fixed, and one poster sees the same in the Steam version. Forum posts, so a weak host.
- **Not verified:** whether the PlayStation and Xbox versions also show the list in-game, or only through the console's trophies and achievements. Pure Completion is on every platform's list either way, so the one row the guide shows exists for every reader. Where the game shows its list in its menus (one ResetEra post says from the main menu) rests on one host, so the row doesn't say.

## The overall figure

The bar counts every board target, each item once and up to its goal (core's default rule), so it reads 100 only when What's left is empty, and under 100 with one Grub left. Core's `overall.test.tsx` pins the first and `guide.test.ts` the second. The Check against the game panel keeps the game's own figure: its points over 112, rounded once (Mask Shards in fours and Vessel Fragments in threes first). The weights are the game's own code: equipment 2, everything else 1. That the figure the inventory shows with World Sense is that same count is the wiki's and the code's, not seen in a save.

## What the bar counts beyond the game's own list

The game's 112% is the first sixteen targets. The rest of the board is what a full save also holds (with the relics, which go in chapter by chapter as a guide list each until the last relic chapter gives them the game's totals: see the next section), and five lists that are the guide's own, whose goals are their rows and not a total any source states (so there are no two hosts for them, and none is claimed): 8 key pickups (`pickup`), 6 bosses outside the game's percentage (`other-boss`: the Crystal Guardian, Flukemarm, Massive Moss Charger, Enraged Guardian, the Hollow Knight and the Radiance, a second tag on rows that were `boss` alone), 4 challenges (`challenge`, two of which, "Practice troublesome bosses" and "Track Pantheon bindings", are practice rows with no end the game marks), the Pure Completion achievement (`achievement`) and 44 quest steps (`quest`; two of the 44, the Nailsmith's two answers, are the ways of a one-way choice, below. The four quest steps that are also a game-counted item, Grimmchild, the first Grimm fight, the Troupe's finale and the City Nailsmith's nail, are not among them: each counts once, as that item, and no longer carries `quest`, so no row counts under two targets). Core's `data.test.ts` holds each goal to its tag's rows, so a row added or dropped fails it until the goal moves. Route steps never count. Where the game has fixed lists (Grubs, maps, Roots and the others) the earlier sections give their totals and hosts.

## One-way choices

`data/choices.ts` holds one, and both of its ways count, so 100% takes a second save, as some endings do:

- **The Nailsmith's request** (`quest-nailsmith-sheo` on the first save, then `quest-nailsmith-purity` in chapter 13, A Second Save). After the Pure Nail he asks to be struck down: striking him ends his story there (Purity), walking away sends him to Sheo's hut (Happy Couple). Both outcomes are on the [wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Nailsmith) and [fextralife](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Nailsmith) (the choice keeps the two hosts its missable entry had). The second save's cost, six Pale Ore and 7,050 Geo (250, 800, 2,000, 4,000), is on the [wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Pale_Ore) and [fextralife](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Pale+Ore); that he falls into the water below is the wiki's. **Barely two hosts:** that the game saves the moment you strike him, so the two answers can't both be had on one save, is the wiki's (hollowknight.fandom.com has the same text, as the wiki is a fork of it), and a 2018 Steam forum reply says the same with nothing cited; fextralife doesn't say it. It is why the other answer is in a second save's chapter, but no row says it. Both pages say his body turns up later in the Junk Pit; the row doesn't go that far.
- Zote is not here: he was ruled a missable, not a choice (his rescue is a matter of timing, and the rows can all be done on one save), so his rows (`quest-zote-greenpath`, `quest-zote-deepnest`, `quest-zote-arena`, and the dream rematch `dream-boss-grey-prince-zote` that needs him beaten in the Colosseum) are a missable and count, flagged in Don't miss; a save that skipped the Greenpath rescue can't reach 100%.

Seen and kept as they were: the Troupe's finale by ritual or banishment (`troupe-finale-grimm-finale`, one row that counts whichever way it ends), the Hollow Knight, Sealed Siblings, Dream No More and Embrace the Void endings and the Delicate Flower one (`ending`, counted on purpose), Cloth (`quest-cloth-basin`, `quest-cloth-gardens`, `quest-cloth-farewell`: found in the Basin she fights beside you, otherwise she goes to Dirtmouth, and only the first branch has rows), Divine (`quest-divine`: before any banishment), the Arcane Egg before the Lifeblood Core (`pickup-lifeblood-egg`), and the Godseeker's flower after Embrace the Void (`quest-godseeker-flower`).
