## Achievements: a source, never shown

Dark Souls Remastered keeps no achievement list of its own. Its 41 achievements are the platforms' (Steam, Xbox, PlayStation trophies), and the Switch version has none (one host, below), so since 19 November 2026 the guide shows none: no target, no category, no chip and no achievement named in a row (the rule is core's, `data.test.ts`, and `guide.test.ts` pins it here, Knight’s Honor included). Steam’s list stays a source, the way the counts above use it to check nothing is missing.

- **No achievements on Switch.** One host says it outright: [gamepressure’s trophy list](https://www.gamepressure.com/darksouls/trophies/zfaf3e) (read directly) says they can be earned on every platform but the Switch, which has no system for them. The user said the same when making the rule (19 November 2026). No page found names an in-game list on any platform: fextralife’s trophy guide (read directly) and Steam’s list give the same 41 platform awards, and neither mentions the Switch. To settle it with a second host: a Switch review or Nintendo’s store page that says so.
- **What became of the 41 rows.** No id changed, and one row was dropped. The 29 that are also a boss, covenant, ring, maximum reinforcement or ending count once, as that (they used to count twice). The 7 route steps (the first bonfire, the class gear, Firelink, both bells, the Lordvessel and Anor Londo) are directions now and count nothing. Of the 5 that were only achievements, 4 (`achievement-knights-honor`, `-wisdom-of-a-sage`, `-bond-of-a-pyromancer`, `-prayer-of-a-maiden`) are the last route steps in chapter 13, worded as checks of the rare weapons and the three spell schools, which their own targets already count. The fifth, `achievement-the-dark-soul` (every other achievement), was dropped on the user's decision (19 November 2026): it was a platform award and nothing more, and a reader's old tick on it is ignored: saved checks for an unknown row are dropped on load, and a synced ledger keeps it without applying it. So the id is retired: a new row must never take it, or synced readers would find it already ticked.

## One-way choices

`data/choices.ts` holds six. The first cycle takes the way the guide recommends, and the other way is a row in chapter 13, for NG+ or NG++. Both count. Where the other way only yields an item, the item's own row is the later way, so the item counts once (since 19 Nov 2026). Each choice's exclusion rests on two hosts (read 19 Nov 2026):

- **Frampt or Kaathe** (`quest-lordvessel-frampt`, then `cycle-ng-plus-plus-kaathe` in NG++). If Kaathe takes you to place the Lordvessel, Frampt leaves until the next playthrough ([wikidot Kaathe](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkstalker-kaathe), [fextralife Frampt](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Kingseeker+Frampt)); that Kaathe leaves when Frampt places it is wikidot's alone. Kaathe's way needs this cycle's Covenant of Artorias: Sif drops it each cycle ([fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covenant+of+Artorias)). Kaathe's way is in NG++ because NG+ can't take it and keep the young Sif scene: Kaathe needs the Kings dead before the vessel is placed, the Kings need the Covenant of Artorias, which doesn't carry over ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/covenant-of-artorias), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covenant+of+Artorias)) and comes from adult Sif, and the young Sif scene needs the Archives, which open only once the vessel is placed. The serpent doesn't decide the ending: [wikidot's Kiln page](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/kiln-of-the-first-flame) and [fextralife's Endings](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Endings) set both after Gwyn. **Not settled:** joining the Darkwraiths and keeping Frampt (`covenant-darkwraith`, `quest-lordvessel-frampt`) rests on one host, on two of its pages: [wikidot Kaathe](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkstalker-kaathe) and [wikidot Darkwraith](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkwraith) give the Homeward route, then the vessel placed with Frampt, who stays, and the membership stays too. [fextralife Darkwraith](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Darkwraith) alone says Frampt disappears once you join; fextralife's own [Lordvessel](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lordvessel) and Frampt pages tie his leaving to Kaathe being at the altar when the vessel goes in, which the route never does. fextralife's [Bugs and Glitches](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Bugs+and+Glitches) (PC, untested) reports Frampt leaving when the vessel is placed by jumping down his pit after a Homeward exit; the route has Frampt carry you. Re-read 19 Nov 2026: Gold Phantom says nothing of Frampt, and fandom refused (402). Read again on 20 Nov 2026, and still not settled: four Steam forum threads (players' posts on one host, which disagree) and one unread snippet. In [one thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/570940/discussions/0/2918850377435762150/) two posters describe the Homeward Bone exit, then placing the vessel with Frampt, which keeps the membership while Kaathe leaves, so Frampt staying is implied and his shop and shards are never named (one of them cites the wikidot Kaathe page, so isn't independent of it). In [another](https://steamcommunity.com/app/570940/discussions/0/1696049513776557658/) one poster says Frampt stays after the Homeward Bone route and neither serpent gets angry, and another says Frampt got angry and left both before and after the vessel (others guess he leaves when Kaathe is angered). In [a third](https://steamcommunity.com/app/211420/discussions/0/810938811036611570) a poster joins first by Homeward Bone and talks to Frampt afterwards without trouble, while others say talking to Frampt with the vessel makes Kaathe vanish. In [a fourth](https://steamcommunity.com/app/211420/discussions/0/620712999982136831/) one poster says placing the vessel makes Frampt disappear, with no Homeward Bone in it (the Kaathe-at-the-altar route). A GameFAQs thread on ranking up as a Darkwraith and keeping Frampt's devouring ([thread 64264020](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/667257-dark-souls-prepare-to-die-edition/64264020)) was seen through a search summary only (403 to the fetcher), which says the Homeward Bone route keeps it; unread. Gamepressure, Gameranx, vgkami and GamesRadar say only not to speak to Frampt or place the vessel before joining, and say nothing of him afterwards. So the route still rests on wikidot's two pages, with forum posters on both sides and one unread snippet; no page read names his shop or shard-splitting after the vessel is placed with his help. To settle it: a second host for the route, or a playthrough. Your current covenant and its rank carry into NG+ ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/new-game-plus), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/New+Game+Plus)); `cycle-ng-plus-sif` said the Darkwraith membership still counts there, which holds only if you end NG a Darkwraith, and no longer says it.
- **Lautrec and Anastacia** (`quest-lautrec-invasion`, then `cycle-ng-plus-lautrec`). He kills her once the second bell is rung or the Parish and Blighttown Fire Keeper Souls are both taken, unless he's killed first at Firelink ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/knight-lautrec-of-carim), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Knight+Lautrec)). Killed early he drops the Ring of Favor and Protection and Humanity, not his armour (fextralife). Refusing gives Twin Humanities ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/patches), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Patches+the+Hyena)); that forgiving sets prism stones toward a hidden bonfire is wikidot's alone. Both put the cleric question before he kicks you into the pit, and `quest-patches-forgive` now says so. **Not verified:** whether he still moves to Firelink after a refusal; no host ties the move to the answer, and none says he moves after one. The first cycle forgives him, so `quest-patches` and the Crescent Axe don't rest on it.
- **Solaire and the red-eyed bug** (`quest-solaire-shortcut`, then `cycle-ng-plus-solaire`; it was a missable until it became a choice). Left alive when you enter Lost Izalith, the bug sends Solaire mad (wikidot adds the Chaos shortcut as a way in that does it too), and he attacks near the Bed of Chaos fog; his sign at Gwyn is gone ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/solaire-of-astora), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Solaire+of+Astora); the choice keeps the missable's [Gold Phantom](https://www.goldphantom.com/tools/ds1/npc-quest-tracker/) source). **Not verified:** what he drops when killed mad, and whether he drops the Sunlight Maggot; the row claims neither. `quest-solaire-saved` follows the recommended branch.
- **How Siegmeyer leaves the pit** (`quest-sieg-ash`, then `ring-speckled-stoneplate` in NG+, since 19 Nov 2026; `cycle-ng-plus-siegmeyer`, the later way until then, is the route step before it). At half health or more he goes to Ash Lake with Sieglinde (the slab), below half he dies and drops the Speckled Stoneplate Ring, and the ring and the slab exclude each other ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/siegmeyer-of-catarina), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Siegmeyer+of+Catarina); [Gold Phantom's NPC tracker](https://www.goldphantom.com/tools/ds1/npc-quest-tracker/) says the ring "rules out" the slab). Killed at any earlier step he drops the ring too, which also ends his story before Ash Lake; killed at Ash Lake after the slab, no page read says. With him at the edge, killing all four Chaos Eaters before you speak to him gets the ring from him (wikidot, fextralife, Gold Phantom). **Exactly half:** wikidot (its Siegmeyer and ring pages) and [TheGamer's questline guide](https://www.thegamer.com/dark-souls-siegmeyer-questline-guide/) say half or more goes to Ash Lake; fextralife says more than half and doesn't rule on exactly half. The rows say half or more, and the choice too since 19 Nov 2026 (it said "above half"). **Chaos Eaters dead before he arrives:** wikidot says only that you can't finish his story, since they don't come back; fextralife says you're left with his ring. Since 19 Nov 2026 the rows say only that the Chaos Eaters don't come back (both hosts), so wait for him at the edge; until then the ring row said an early kill ended his story "with nothing", which neither says.
- **Ciaran and the Soul of Artorias** (`quest-ciaran`, then `dlc-abyss-greatsword` in NG+, since 19 Nov 2026; `cycle-ng-plus-dlc`, the later way until then, is the route step before it). Handed over, the soul is hers for the Tracers ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/lord-s-blade-ciaran), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lord's+Blade+Ciaran)); kept, it makes the Abyss Greatsword ([wikidot Soul of Artorias](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/soul-of-artorias), not on the choice's two pages). She asks again in NG+ whenever the soul is carried into the arena (wikidot). **Disagree:** one refusal and she's gone (wikidot), or two (Gold Phantom). Killing her keeps the soul and drops both Tracers, so the choice isn't strictly one-way; the guide doesn't route it.

## Builds

`data/build-plan.ts` points a build at rows; it counts nothing. How its figures are written ([chapter-data.md](../../../docs/chapter-data.md#builds)):

- **No scaling letters.** wikidot's [reinforcement formulas](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/reinforcement-formulas) put C at 52–79 and fextralife's [Weapon Scaling](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Weapon+Scaling) at 50–74, and a +0 letter rises with each upgrade, so a pick says which stat a weapon grows with.
- **Requirements are one-handed** (a pick's `needs`), as each weapon page's table gives them; its footnote gives two-handed Strength, which counts 1.5× ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/strength), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Strength)), so 24 Strength is 16 in both hands, 28 is 19, 46 is 31 and 16 is 11.
- **Leanings.** Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence and Faith set every requirement and scaling; Pyromancy grows only with the flame ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/pyromancy-flame), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Pyromancy+Flame)). Attunement is left out as a leaning on purpose: every caster needs its slots. Humanity was a leaning until 21 Nov 2026 (below).

## Route decisions

- NG joins the Darkwraiths without placing the Lordvessel for Kaathe: he carries you to the altar, you leave with a Homeward Bone, join him in the Abyss, then place the vessel with Frampt, who keeps his services ([wikidot Kaathe](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkstalker-kaathe)). The Dark Lord ending needs no covenant.
- NG kills Ornstein first, so Smough’s soul; NG+ kills Smough first, for Ornstein’s soul and the Leo Ring. Either order works across two cycles; Gold Phantom suggests the reverse.
- A boss leaves one soul a cycle. NG makes the Moonlight Butterfly Horn, Quelaag’s Furysword, the Golem Axe, the Greatsword of Artorias (from the hilt), Smough’s Hammer and the Darkmoon Bow; NG+ the Crystal Ring Shield, Chaos Blade, Dragon Bone Fist, cursed Greatsword of Artorias, Dragonslayer Spear and Tin Darkmoon Catalyst; NG++ the Greatshield of Artorias and the Great Lord Greatsword. A pair swaps freely, which chapter 11's forging note and `cycle-ng-plus-anor` say.
- **Later-cycle items sit in chapter 13 (19 Nov 2026).** Until then the twelve a later cycle gives (the six NG+ recipes above, the two NG++ ones, the Abyss Greatsword, the Manus Catalyst, the Leo Ring and the Speckled Stoneplate Ring) sat in chapter 11's sweep, where Next up offered them during NG, and five were counted twice beside the chapter 13 step that yields them. Each moved with its id unchanged, so no tick is lost, and counts once, there. By the completion rules (docs/core.md, rules 2 and 4), ten of chapter 13's fourteen cycle rows became route steps: the bells, the Lord Souls, the trip to the smith and young Sif's rescue only repeat something (her alternate introduction is a scene the game doesn't credit, and `quest-young-sif` counts the rescue), and the Ornstein and Smough, Gwyndolin, Siegmeyer, expansion, Shiva and last-forge steps only yield items their own rows count.
- NG gives Artorias’s soul to Ciaran and uses Manus’s soul for Pursuers; NG+ forges the Abyss Greatsword and the Manus Catalyst.
- NG+ rescues young Sif before adult Sif. A Darkwraith cycle can’t: the Four Kings need adult Sif’s ring before the Lordvessel goes in, and the Broken Pendant is in the Archives behind it.
- Siegmeyer: the Ash Lake branch in NG, the Speckled Stoneplate Ring in NG+.
- NG’s Gwyn soul becomes Sunlight Spear in NG+; NG+’s becomes the Great Lord Greatsword in NG++, which takes the third Sif soul and the last forge, then Kaathe's way with the Lordvessel.
- Deadline-bound spells sit in their vendor’s chapter (Laurentius in 3, Siegmeyer’s Emit Force in 5, Logan in 7, Reah and the Gravelord dances in 8, Quelana in 9). Chapter 11 keeps the spells nobody can lose.
- **The Great Hollow and Ash Lake sit in chapter 5, not chapter 3 (22 Nov 2026).** Without the Lordvessel, leaving Ash Lake means the long climb back up the Great Hollow, or a Homeward Bone that only helps if you haven't rested below, since it returns you to the last bonfire you rested at ([wikidot Ash Lake](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/ash-lake), [wikidot Homeward Bone](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/homeward-bone)). [fextralife's Great Hollow page](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/The+Great+Hollow) advises getting the Lordvessel first, [its route page](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Game+Progress+Route) and [the Steam guide 2673148886](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2673148886) leave the area for later, and [wikidot's Great Hollow page](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/the-great-hollow) describes a collision bug on the climb. So the five rows moved, ids unchanged, beside Siegmeyer's swamp visit, which already stands by the big tree: you go down once, light both bonfires and warp out. The total doesn't change; chapter 3 counts five rows fewer and chapter 5 five more.

### Picks and steps (`build-plan.ts`, 21 Nov 2026)
