# Facts to verify

What this guide claims that nobody has confirmed in the game. Nothing here was replayed. The collectibles were read on 24 November 2026 from [100% Guides' list of all 282](https://www.100pguides.com/guides/mina-the-hollower-all-collectible-locations) (one page, every area), [NiaMeowDB's checklist](https://meowdb.com/db/mina-the-hollower/checklist), its [Kear](https://meowdb.com/db/mina-the-hollower/kears) and [Bonestone](https://meowdb.com/db/mina-the-hollower/bonestone-chests) lists and its [boss list](https://meowdb.com/db/mina-the-hollower/bosses), and Gamer Guides' [trinket](https://www.gamerguides.com/mina-the-hollower/guide/collectibles/trinkets/all-trinket-locations), [Joule Box](https://www.gamerguides.com/mina-the-hollower/guide/collectibles/upgrades/all-joule-box-locations) and [Trinket Bag](https://www.gamerguides.com/mina-the-hollower/guide/collectibles/upgrades/all-trinket-bag-locations) lists. The route started from an editorial draft whose randomizer citations are not used as evidence of what the game holds.
